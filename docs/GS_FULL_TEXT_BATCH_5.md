- §99: "would probably have said" (*vielleicht*) and "perhaps very unfortunate";

- §108: the caves (*vielleicht*);
- §112: "probably" (*wahrscheinlich*).

Unhedged claims are marked as such, for example §111's "Undoubtedly".
